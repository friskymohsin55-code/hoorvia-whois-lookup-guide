How to Read a WHOIS Record Before You Buy a Domain

Before you pay for a domain, spend thirty seconds on its public record. A free WHOIS lookup tells you who holds the name, how old it is, when it expires, and whether it can even be transferred. That is the whole story of the domain, straight from the registry.

A real record, read line by line

Run the lookup on example.com and the record comes back like this:

FieldRecord value
Domain nameEXAMPLE.COM
RegistrarRESERVED-Internet Assigned Numbers Authority
DatesCreated 1995-08-14, updated 2026-08-14, expiry 2027-08-13
StatusesClient delete prohibited, client transfer prohibited, client update prohibited
Name serversELLIOTT.NS.CLOUDFLARE.COM, HERA.NS.CLOUDFLARE.COM

Three dates answer three questions: when the name began, when it last changed, and when the registration ends. The statuses say the domain is locked down — it cannot be deleted, transferred, or changed without the registrar unlocking it first. The name servers show its DNS is handled by Cloudflare. The data comes through RDAP, the modern registry protocol, from the Verisign RDAP service itself — not a stale copy.

What to check before you buy

First, read the registrar: that is the company you will deal with for renewals, transfers, and disputes. Second, check the expiry date: a name expiring next week can be lost before a deal closes, so renew first. Third, read the statuses: "client transfer prohibited" means the seller must unlock the domain before you pay, or the move will fail on the day. Fourth, check the creation date to judge the domain's age — a name registered last month carries no history to inherit.

Rule of thumb: if the expiry date is weeks away and the transfer lock is on, the domain is safe but locked. Renew it, unlock it at the registrar, then start the transfer — in that order.

One more check after the WHOIS record: a domain's age is only part of its value. Run its domain authority to see how strong its backlink profile is, and check who links to it with a backlink checker. Age plus authority plus a clean link profile is what makes a domain worth buying. All three checks are free, with no signup needed.
